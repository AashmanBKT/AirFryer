Welcome to the definitive **100-Recipe Air Fryer Cookbook**, crafted exclusively for rapid cyclonic convection cooking. Every single recipe is engineered for peak crispiness, rich authentic flavors, and up to 85% less dietary fat compared to traditional deep frying.

   - **Instant Search:** Search by recipe name, ingredient (*paneer, corn, potato, dal, mawa*), or tags.
   - **Category Filters:** Switch seamlessly between **All (100)**, **Breakfast (20)**, **Snacks & Starters (40)**, **Main Course (24)**, and **Sweet Treats (16)**.
   - **Temperature Unit Toggle:** Switch all 100 recipes between **°C** and **°F** with one click.
   - **Interactive Recipe Drawer:**
     - **Servings Scaler:** Scale portions from 2 to 16 servings with automatic ingredient calculation.
     - **Built-in Basket Timer:** Live countdown timer with pause, reset, and doneness chime.
     - **Interactive Prep Checklist:** Check off ingredients and recipe steps as you cook.
